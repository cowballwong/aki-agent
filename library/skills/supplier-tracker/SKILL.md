# Supplier and subcontractor tracker

## The table

One row per package or item: what · who was asked · who replied · price ·
lead time · appointed · status. Keep it in the job's own folder, not in your
head and not in an email thread.

## Comparing quotes

Line them up on **the same scope**. Most quotation comparisons are wrong
because two of the three priced something different — say so explicitly when
they have, rather than presenting a total that is not comparable.

Show: price, what is excluded, lead time, and what each one assumed.

## Chasing

List what is late, by how long, and what it is holding up. That last part is
what makes a chaser effective — "we need it Thursday or the pour slips" is a
different message from "please advise".

## What this never does

It does not appoint anybody, place an order, or send a purchase instruction.
It prepares those and stops.
