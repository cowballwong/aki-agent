# Client preference and history record

## Per client

Last visit and what was done · products used · what they liked and disliked ·
anything to avoid, including sensitivities and allergies · notes they have
mentioned about themselves.

## Why this is the whole business

A regular who is asked the same questions every visit is a regular who is
considering somewhere else. A record that says "prefers no fragrance, allergic
to X, mentioned wedding in November" is the difference.

## Rules

- **Anything to avoid goes at the top, always.** Never buried in history.
- Record what they said, not judgements about them.
- Personal notes are for service, not for gossip — write nothing you would
  not want the client to read, because one day they might.

## Personal data

Stays on this machine. Contact and health-adjacent details are never copied
into a marketing list without their consent being recorded.
