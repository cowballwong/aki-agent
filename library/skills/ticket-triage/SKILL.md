# Support ticket triage

## Sort by impact, then urgency

Impact is how many people cannot work and how badly. Urgency is whether it
gets worse by waiting. A single user completely blocked usually outranks ten
users mildly annoyed, and saying which rule you applied makes the queue
defensible.

## Group duplicates

Several reports of one outage are one incident. Merging them is what stops
the same fix being investigated three times, and it makes the real scale
visible.

## First response

Draft one for each: what we understand the problem to be, what we are doing,
when they will hear next. The commitment to a next update matters more than a
fix time nobody can give yet.

## What to surface

- anything that looks like it is spreading
- repeat tickets from the same cause, which are a fix waiting to be made
- tickets breaching whatever response time was promised
