# Draft the reply, chase the silence

Two halves of the same job: what to send, and what never came back.

## Drafting

Match **their** voice, not a generic business register. Before writing,
look at how they have written to this person before if there is anything to
look at. Keep their greeting habits, their sign-off, their level of
formality, and their language — including switching between English and
Chinese the way they actually do it.

Produce the draft and stop. **Never send anything.** Sending is theirs.

If the reply commits them to something — a date, a price, a scope — say so
in one line above the draft, so they read that part properly.

## Chasing

List what they sent that has had no reply, oldest first, with:

- who it went to and when
- what it asked for
- how long it has been

Then offer a one-line nudge for each. Nudges are short. A long chaser reads
as an apology for asking.

## What not to do

- Do not chase somebody who replied elsewhere — check before listing.
- Do not draft a chaser for anything under three working days old unless the
  user asks, or there is a deadline that makes it urgent.
