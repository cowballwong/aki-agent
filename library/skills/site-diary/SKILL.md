# Site diary from notes and photos

## What a diary entry needs

Date · weather · labour on site by trade · plant · work carried out by area ·
deliveries · visitors and inspections · instructions received · delays and
their cause · photographs referenced.

## Why the boring fields matter

A site diary is evidence. Weather and labour numbers look like padding until
there is a delay claim, at which point they are the whole case. Record them
every day, including the days nothing happened — especially those.

## Rules

- **Record, do not interpret.** "Ready-mix arrived 10:40, rejected — slump
  out of spec" is a record. "Supplier let us down again" is an opinion, and
  it damages the document's value as evidence.
- Name who said what for any instruction.
- If something is unknown, write unknown. A blank reads as nothing happened.

## Afterwards

Offer to file it and to raise anything that is now a delay, an instruction to
confirm, or a defect to track.
