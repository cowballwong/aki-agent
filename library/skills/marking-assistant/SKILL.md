# Marking assistance with a rubric

## What this is

A first pass that shows its working, for a teacher to check and override. It
is not the mark. Say that on every output.

## Method

For each criterion in the rubric: the evidence from the work, quoted, and the
band it fits. Where a piece sits between bands, say so and say why — that is
where the teacher's judgement is actually needed and it should be surfaced,
not averaged away.

## Feedback

Two things done well, one thing to do next, in the student's language and at
their level. Specific to their work, quoting it.

## Rules

- Never mark without the rubric. Ask for it.
- Flag anything that reads as not the student's own work as a **question for
  the teacher** — never an accusation, and never a conclusion.
- Keep the same standard across the pile: mark criterion by criterion across
  all scripts rather than script by script, which is how drift happens.
