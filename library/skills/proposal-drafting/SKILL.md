# Proposal drafting

## The shape that wins work

1. **What we understood** — their problem in their words. If this section is
   wrong, nothing else is read.
2. **What we would do** — concretely, in stages, with what each stage
   produces.
3. **What we would need from them** — and when. Underrated, and it prevents
   the delay that gets blamed on you.
4. **What it costs**, by stage, with what is excluded.
5. **Who does it** and why they are the right people.

## Rules

- Reuse from previous proposals, but never leave another client's name,
  scope or numbers in. Check specifically for this before it goes.
- Price the stages so a client can stop between them. A proposal that can
  only be accepted whole is often not accepted.
- State how long the price holds.
