# Insurance claim form preparation

## What to do

Take the insurer's own form and fill what the records support, field by
field. List separately what the form asks for that the records do not
contain.

## Rules

- **Copy from the record, never from memory of the consultation.** Dates,
  codes, amounts and names come from the file.
- Where a field needs a clinical judgement — a diagnosis, a code, whether
  something was necessary — leave it for the practitioner. Prepare everything
  around it.
- Never round, adjust or reword an amount so that a form balances.

## Personal data

This is health and financial information about a named person. It stays on
this machine, it is not pasted into an email draft, and reference numbers are
redacted from anything that leaves the file.

## Output

The filled form, the list of blanks with who must complete each, and anything
in the record that appears inconsistent — flagged, not corrected.
