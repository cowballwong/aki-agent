# Revision round tracker

## Per deliverable

Round number · what was asked · who asked · date · what was included in the
fee · whether this round is within scope.

## Why this is the tracker that pays for itself

Scope creep is not a single event; it is the fourth round of small changes
nobody counted. A record that shows "this is round 5; the fee covered 2" turns
an awkward conversation into a factual one, at the time rather than at the
invoice.

## Rules

- Record the request in the client's own words.
- Flag the moment a round exceeds what was agreed — not later, when it is
  three rounds past and the relationship is strained.
- Distinguish a correction of our error (not chargeable, and say so) from a
  change of mind (chargeable). Being scrupulous about the first is what makes
  the second acceptable.
