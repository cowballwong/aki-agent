# Roster and leave

## Planning a roster

Start from what must be covered — opening hours, minimum staffing, anything
that legally needs a qualified person present. Then fit people to it, and
show what is uncovered rather than quietly leaving a hole.

Respect what the user has told you about people: who cannot work certain
days, who is training, who has asked for something.

## Leave

Track requested · approved · taken · remaining. Keep statutory entitlement
separate from anything the employer gives on top — they behave differently
when somebody leaves.

In Hong Kong, an employer must keep wage and employment records for the
preceding 12 months for each employee. **Ask** whether the user is the
employer and wants that kept here; do not assume it.

## Rules

- Never approve leave. Prepare it for the person who decides.
- Flag a clash the moment it appears, not when the roster is published.
- If a change affects somebody's pay, say so in the same breath.
