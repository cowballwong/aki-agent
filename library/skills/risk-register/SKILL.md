# Risk register upkeep

## Each risk

What could happen · what it would cost in money and time · how likely ·
what we are doing about it · who owns it · when it was last looked at.

## The discipline that makes it real

**Review dates.** A register whose entries were last touched at tender is a
document, not a control. Flag anything not reviewed in the period the project
uses, and say what has changed since.

## What to add

New risks come from what actually happened: a late approval, a supplier
wobble, a design change. When one of those appears in the record, offer to add
or update the risk.

## What to close

Risks that have passed, with a line saying what happened. A register that only
grows is one nobody reads.

## Never

Do not invent probabilities. If nobody has assessed it, say unassessed.
