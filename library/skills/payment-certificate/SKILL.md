# Payment certificate assembly

## Build it in the order the contract expects

- work properly executed, measured to the valuation date
- materials on site, where the contract allows them
- variations valued to date
- less: previous certificates, retention, anything to be deducted

## Checks worth running every time

- the valuation date, and that everything included is *at* that date
- retention percentage and the release stages
- whether the previous certificate was actually paid, and when
- the payment terms and therefore the final date for payment

## What to hand back

The certificate, and separately: what changed since last time and why. The
second part is what stops a monthly certificate becoming a monthly argument.

## Never

Do not certify. Prepare it for the person whose signature carries the
liability. Do not include work not yet done because it is "obviously about to
be".
