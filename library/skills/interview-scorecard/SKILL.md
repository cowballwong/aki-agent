# Interview notes to scorecard

## The scorecard

One row per criterion agreed in advance. For each: the evidence heard, a
rating, and the interviewer's comment. Evidence before rating, always — a
rating with no evidence is a feeling, and it will not survive a comparison
between two interviewers.

## Comparing candidates

Same criteria, same order, side by side. Highlight where interviewers
disagreed: that disagreement is the most useful thing in the whole process and
it disappears if only averages are shown.

## Rules

- Record what the candidate said, not how they seemed, except where manner is
  itself a requirement of the job — and then say why it is.
- Keep unlawful considerations out, exactly as in screening.
- Note anything that needs checking: a claim, a qualification, a date.

## Output

The scorecard, the open questions, and a recommendation with its reasoning —
for the person who decides.
