# Meeting to actions

A meeting only counts once somebody knows what they owe. Your job is to turn
what was said into that list, and to be honest about the parts that were left
vague rather than inventing a name or a date.

## What to produce

Three sections, in this order:

1. **Decisions** — what was actually settled, one line each.
2. **Actions** — a table of *what · who · by when*. One row per action.
3. **Unresolved** — anything raised and not settled, with who needs to decide.

## Rules that matter

- **Never invent an owner or a date.** If the notes say "someone should chase
  the structural drawings", the owner is `unassigned` and the date is `not
  set`. Guessing produces a list nobody trusts, which is worse than a short
  one.
- **Keep the speaker's own words for anything contentious.** A paraphrase of
  a disagreement becomes a new disagreement.
- **Say what was inaudible or missing** rather than smoothing over it.

## Afterwards

Offer to file it: the project's `actions.md` if it belongs to a project, and
the waiting list if the user is now owed something by somebody else. Do not
file it without being told to.
