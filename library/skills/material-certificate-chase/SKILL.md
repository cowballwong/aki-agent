# Material certificate and test chase

## The register

For each element or pour: what the specification requires · what has been
received · reference and date · pass or fail · what is outstanding.

## What to watch

- **Missing before it matters.** A certificate outstanding at handover is a
  problem; the same one outstanding before covering up is a phone call.
- **Failed and retested** — keep both. A register showing only passes is not a
  record, and reads badly to anyone reviewing it later.
- **Certificates that do not match the delivery** — batch, date, grade.

## Reporting

Outstanding items first, with what they hold up. Then anything failed and its
resolution. Then the complete set, for the record.

## Never

Never mark something received on the strength of somebody saying it was sent.
The register records documents held, not documents promised.
