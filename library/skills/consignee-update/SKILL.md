# Consignment status update

## What a customer actually wants

Where it is · whether the date changed · what they should do differently if
so. Not a copy of the carrier's event log.

## Writing it

Lead with the answer. "Still on schedule, arriving Tuesday 14th" or "Delayed
two days, now Thursday 16th — your delivery slot needs moving."

If something has gone wrong, say what is being done and when the next update
comes. A promised next update is what stops the customer chasing daily.

## When there is no news

Say that, and say when you will chase. Silence is read as a problem.

## Watch

- shipments with no movement for longer than usual for that route
- anything approaching free-time expiry at either end, where waiting costs
  money by the day
