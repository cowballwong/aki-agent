# Clash and coordination log

## Each entry

Location · what clashes with what · severity · who owns it · proposed
resolution · status · date raised and closed.

## Severity, honestly

Rank by what it costs to fix later, not by how it looks in the model. A duct
through a beam is severe. Two containment routes 30mm apart is noise, and a
log full of noise is a log nobody opens.

## Running a review

Take them in order of severity, record the decision and **who made it** — a
resolution with no named decision-maker gets reopened at the next meeting.

## What to surface weekly

- unresolved items that block a fabrication or an order
- items reopened more than once, which usually means the real disagreement
  has not been named
