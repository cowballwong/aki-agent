# Asset library keeper

## The structure

Per client, per project: **working** · **final** · **delivered**. Delivered is
never edited — it is what the client has, and it is the answer to "what did we
actually send".

## Naming

`client — project — asset — version — date`. Version numbers, not "final",
"final2", "final-USE-THIS". A version scheme that survives contact with a
deadline is one where the newest is unambiguous by sorting.

## What to keep

Source files with their fonts and links, the export, and the brief the asset
was made for. The last one is what makes an asset reusable a year later.

## What to flag

- finals with no matching source
- assets using a licensed font or image, and where that licence stands
- anything delivered that was never filed as delivered
