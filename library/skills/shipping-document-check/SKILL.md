# Shipping document set check

## Cross-check, field by field

Consignee, shipper, notify party, description of goods, quantity, weight,
value, incoterm, port of loading and discharge, marks and numbers — the same
on every document in the set.

**A mismatch between the invoice and the bill of lading is the single most
common cause of a shipment stopping at the other end.** Finding it before it
sails costs an email; finding it after costs demurrage.

## Completeness

Against what the destination requires for these goods: certificates of
origin, licences, inspection certificates, fumigation, insurance.

## Report

Mismatches first, with both values quoted and where each came from. Then
missing documents with who obtains each.

## Never

Do not classify goods or assign an HS code. Flag where classification is
needed and who decides it.
