# Quote, invoice and chase

## Quoting

Work from the user's own rate card or their previous quotes. If neither
exists, ask for the rates once and remember them.

A quotation has to say what is **not** included as clearly as what is. Most
disputes are about the boundary, not the price.

Include: scope, what is excluded, the basis of the price (lump sum, rates,
time), what it assumes, how long the price holds, and payment terms.

## Invoicing

From the quote and what was actually done. If they differ, say so above the
invoice rather than quietly billing the difference — an invoice a client
queries costs more than the conversation it skipped.

Number invoices in the sequence the user already uses. Never restart or
re-use a number.

## Chasing

List what is outstanding, oldest first, with the age in days and the terms
that applied. Draft the reminder; **do not send it**.

A first reminder is a short, friendly note that assumes it was missed. Escalate
only when the user says so — they know the relationship and you do not.

## Never

Do not alter a number the user has given you to make something balance. If
the figures do not add up, say which two disagree.
