# Customer review replies

## Good reviews

Short, specific, human. Thank them for the thing they actually mentioned.
Three identical thank-yous in a row tell every reader they are automated.

## Bad reviews

Reply for the hundred people who will read it, not to win the argument with
one.

- acknowledge what they experienced, without conceding what did not happen
- say what is being done
- move it off the platform: a name, a phone number, an invitation to
  contact directly

Never argue, never blame the customer, never mention what you know about their
account in public. Never disclose that somebody was a patient, a client or a
customer if the review does not already say so.

## Rules

- Drafted only. A public reply is sent by the owner, who takes the risk.
- Where a review alleges something serious — safety, hygiene, discrimination —
  flag it for a considered response, not a quick one.
