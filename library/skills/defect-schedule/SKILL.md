# Defect schedule

## Each defect

Reference · location, precisely · what is wrong, observed not diagnosed ·
photograph · who is responsible · required rectification · target date ·
status.

## Precision in location

"Unit 8/F A, master bedroom, west wall, 1.2m from window" — a defect that
cannot be found again is a defect that will be argued about.

## Observed, not diagnosed

"Hairline crack, 300mm, above door head" is what you saw. Whether it is
settlement or shrinkage is a diagnosis, and it belongs in a separate,
labelled line — because a wrong diagnosis in a defect schedule gets quoted
back at you.

## Close-out

Re-inspect and record who verified it and when. Items closed without
re-inspection are the reason second and third schedules exist.

## Output

Open items by responsibility, so each party gets their own list, plus the
full register for the record.
