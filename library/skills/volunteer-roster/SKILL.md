# Volunteer roster and thanks

## Rostering

Cover what must be covered, then fit people to it, respecting what they have
said about availability, skills and what they do not want to do. Show
uncovered slots rather than assuming somebody will appear.

Volunteers are not staff: build in more cover than you need, and confirm
closer to the date.

## Tracking

Who served, when, and at what. This is not bureaucracy — it is what makes the
thanks specific and it is what shows, over a year, who is quietly carrying
too much.

## Thanks

Draft it specific and prompt. "Thanks for covering two Saturdays in a row when
we were short" is worth ten circular notes of appreciation.

## What to surface

- people rostered too often, before they burn out
- people who volunteered and have never been asked
- slots that fail repeatedly, which is a structural problem, not a people one
