# Licence and permit renewal watch

## The register

Licence · authority · number · issued · expires · what renewal requires · who
owns it.

## What renewal requires

This is the column that matters. Some renewals are a fee; some need an
inspection, a certificate, a fire services confirmation, or a landlord's
consent — each with its own lead time. Record it once, from the renewal notice
or the authority's own requirement, and the annual panic disappears.

## Warning

Warn by lead time, not by a fixed number of days: a licence needing an
inspection needs months.

## Rules

- Never state a licence requirement from memory. Work from the licence, the
  notice, or the authority's published requirement, and say which.
- Trading without a current licence is not a paperwork problem, so mark
  anything approaching expiry as urgent, prominently.
