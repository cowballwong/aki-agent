# Appointment reminder run

## What to produce

For the period asked for: one reminder per appointment, drafted in the
practice's usual wording and language, plus a list of problems worth a human
look.

## The problems worth flagging

- **gaps** — unfilled slots, with how long each is, so somebody can decide
  whether to fill them
- **double bookings** and anything overlapping
- **first visits**, which usually need something extra sent beforehand
- **repeat no-shows**, flagged for a decision about deposits or policy, not
  for a lecture in the reminder

## Personal data

Appointment lists are health information. **They stay on this machine.** Do
not include a condition, a treatment or a reason for the visit in a reminder
message — a name, a date and a time is what a reminder needs, and anything
more can be read by whoever picks up the phone.

## Never

Never send. Prepare, and let the practice send from its own system.
