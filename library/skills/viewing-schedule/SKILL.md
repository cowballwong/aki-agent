# Viewing schedule

## Building the day

Group by area and travel time, not by the order the requests arrived. Allow
realistic gaps — a schedule that assumes ten minutes between districts fails
by the third appointment and costs the whole afternoon.

For each: time, property, who is attending, key or access arrangement,
contact number.

## Access

The most common failure is the key. Record for each property how access is
obtained and who has to be told. Confirm access the day before, separately
from confirming the viewer.

## Confirmations

Draft the confirmations to viewers and to whoever controls access. Send is
theirs.

## After

Prompt for a note on each: who came, what they said, interest level, next
step. Written the same day or it is not written.
