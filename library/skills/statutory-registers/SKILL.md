# Statutory registers and annual filings

## What to keep

The registers the company is required to maintain — directors, secretaries,
members, charges, and any register of controllers where required — each
current as at a date, with the resolution or form that supports every change.

## Changes

Every change has a source document and a filing deadline. Record all three:
what changed, what evidences it, what must be filed and by when.

## The annual cycle

Annual return, financial statements where required, business registration
renewal, and any licence. Each with its own date, from the authority's own
requirement — **not from memory**.

## Rules

- A register entry with no supporting document is an entry that will fail
  inspection.
- Never file anything. Prepare it for the person authorised to sign.
