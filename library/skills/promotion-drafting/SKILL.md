# Promotion and campaign drafting

## The offer, precisely

What is included, what it costs, who can use it, when it starts and ends, how
many, and what happens if they cannot use it. Vagueness here becomes a dispute
at the counter.

## The message

Short, one offer, one action. A message with three offers converts on none.
Produce both language versions where the customer base is bilingual.

## The claims rule

**Every claim must be something the business can evidence.** "Reduces the
appearance of X" is a claim; "cures" or "removes permanently" is a different
kind of statement with legal weight, and health, medical or efficacy claims
are regulated in most markets.

Flag anything that needs evidence, and say what evidence would be needed.

## Consent

Only send to people who agreed to be contacted, and include how to stop.
Draft only; sending is theirs.
