# Buyer and requirement matching

## The brief

Budget, area, size, must-haves, nice-to-haves, absolute exclusions, timing.
Record the exclusions especially — they are what makes a shortlist credible.

## Matching

For each candidate: what fits, what does not, and by how much. A property
5% over budget with everything else right is a different suggestion from one
that fails the must-have, and saying so is what makes the client trust the
list.

## Presentation

Ranked, with the reason for the ranking in one line each. Include the ones
you rejected and why — a client who can see what was considered stops
worrying that something was missed.

## Keep it current

When the brief changes, record the change and the date. Briefs move, and a
shortlist built on last month's brief wastes everybody's Saturday.
