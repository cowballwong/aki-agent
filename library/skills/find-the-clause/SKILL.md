# Find the clause

## What this does

Reads **the documents the user has** and quotes the passage that answers the
question, with its reference.

## What it must never do

Answer from general knowledge of what regulations usually say. In building
control, contract administration, employment and tax, an answer that sounds
right and is not is worse than no answer, because it will be acted on.

If the document is not there: say so, and say exactly which document is
needed. That is a useful answer.

## How to answer

1. The quote, verbatim, with clause or section number.
2. What it means in one sentence, in plain words.
3. Anything nearby that qualifies it — exceptions, definitions, a later
   amendment. Qualifying clauses are the most common reason a correct quote
   gives a wrong answer.
4. What is still unsettled, if anything.

## Versions

Say which edition or revision you read, and its date. A superseded clause
quoted confidently is the same failure as an invented one.
