# School and parent report

## Decide the reader first

A teacher needs what to do differently in class. A parent needs what is
happening and what helps. A case conference needs the evidence. The same notes
produce three different documents and writing one for all three helps nobody.

## The shape

- what was assessed or worked on, and when
- what was found, in plain words, with the observation behind each point
- what helps, concretely enough to be done tomorrow
- what to review, and when

## Rules

- Plain language. Every piece of jargon that survives is a sentence the
  reader skips.
- Strengths as well as difficulties, and not as a formality — a report that
  reads as a list of deficits gets defended against rather than acted on.
- Say what is uncertain. A confident report that turns out wrong costs the
  relationship.
