# Sample and purchase order tracker

## Samples

What was asked for · sent when · received when · verdict · what changed for
the next one. Keep every round: "third sample, still wrong on colour" is a
different conversation from "sample not approved".

## Orders

PO number · supplier · quantity · agreed price · required date · production
status · shipped · received.

## What to watch

- samples awaiting a verdict from us — the delay everybody blames on the
  supplier and is usually ours
- orders in production with no confirmed ship date as the required date
  approaches
- price or quantity changed after the PO, without a revised PO

## Never

Do not approve a sample or issue a PO. Prepare and track.
