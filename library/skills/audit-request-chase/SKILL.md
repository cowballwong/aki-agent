# Audit request list chase

## The register

Item · who holds it · requested date · promised date · received · where it
was filed · auditor's follow-up.

## Running it

Group by who has to produce each item, not by the auditor's numbering. The
person who has to act wants their own list, and an audit list sent whole to
five people gets ignored by all five.

## What to watch

- items received but queried again — those are usually the ones that end up
  in the management letter
- anything requiring a third party (bank, lawyer, valuer), which has a lead
  time nobody controls: chase those first, on day one
- items the client has said do not exist — record that answer, with who said
  it and when

## Output

Outstanding by owner, then a full register for the file.
