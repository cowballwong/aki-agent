# Weekly write-up

## Where the material comes from

The user's own record: what changed in their projects, what was decided, what
is waiting, what got finished. Read it rather than asking them to tell you —
the point of this is that they do not have to reconstruct their own week.

## The shape

- **Done** — what actually moved, with the thing itself, not the effort.
- **Next** — what happens before the next update.
- **Waiting on** — with who, and since when. This is the section that gets a
  report read, because it is the part that asks the reader for something.
- **Risks or decisions needed** — only real ones. A padded risk section
  trains people to skip the whole document.

## Tone

Match the audience. A note to a client is not the note to a site team. Ask
who it is for the first time, then remember it.

## Honesty

If a week was quiet, the report is short. Inflating it is how a status report
stops being read — and this assistant's whole credibility rests on its
reports being worth reading.
