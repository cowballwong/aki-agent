# Funding and grant application

## Start with their own words

The strongest material is already written: their project notes, their last
application, their service records. Draft from those rather than from
generalities about the sector.

## The shape most applications want

- what the need is, with evidence
- what you will do about it, concretely
- who does it and why they can
- what it costs, broken down
- how anyone will know whether it worked

## Rules

- **Every number must trace to something.** A figure invented to fill a box
  is the one an assessor asks about.
- Answer the question asked, in the space given. Assessors read hundreds;
  an over-long answer is scored down by a tired person.
- Keep the applicant's voice. A grant written in perfect consultancy prose
  reads as though somebody else wrote it, because somebody else did.

## Afterwards

Note the reporting obligations that come with the money — dates, format,
evidence — and put them on the deadline list before the money arrives, not
after.
