# Repair quotation comparison

## The first job is making them comparable

Most quotes for the same job are not for the same scope. Line them up item by
item and show what each one included, excluded and assumed. Where one has
priced something the others have not, say so — that is usually the whole
difference in the totals.

## The table

Item · A · B · C · notes. Totals at the bottom, and separately: the total on a
like-for-like basis, which is the only total worth comparing.

## Also compare

Lead time, warranty, whether the price includes making good, who takes the
waste, and what happens if more is found once opened up. That last one is
where a cheap quote becomes the expensive one.

## Never

Do not recommend a contractor. Present the comparison and the questions worth
asking each of them.
