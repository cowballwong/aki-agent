# Board and meeting minutes

## What minutes record

Who was present, that a quorum existed, what was decided, and — where it
matters — that a director declared an interest and how that was handled.

Minutes are not a transcript. Discussion is summarised; **decisions are
recorded precisely**, in the words of the resolution.

## Resolutions

Quote the resolution exactly as passed, with proposer, seconder where the
constitution requires them, and the result. A resolution paraphrased is a
resolution that may not have been passed in the form recorded.

## Rules

- Draft from notes taken at the time. Do not reconstruct a meeting nobody
  minuted without saying that is what this is.
- Flag anything that requires a filing or a register change as a consequence.
- Leave approval and signature to the chair.

## Afterwards

Add any consequential filings and deadlines to the watch list.
