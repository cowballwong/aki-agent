# Home programme sheet

## What makes one get done

Short, specific, and possible on a bad day. Three things done daily beat
eleven things done once.

For each item: what to do, how many, how often, and one line on what it is
for — people continue with things they understand the point of.

## Include

- what "doing it right" looks like, and the most common way it goes wrong
- what to do if it hurts, is too easy, or is too hard
- when to stop and contact the practice

## Language and reading level

Write it for the person who has to follow it, in their language, at a level
they will actually read. Where the user works bilingually, produce both, laid
out side by side.

## Never

Never invent an exercise, a dose or a progression that the practitioner did
not give. This sheet writes down what was prescribed; it does not prescribe.
