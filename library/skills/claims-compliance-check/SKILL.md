# Advertising claims check

## Go through the copy and mark each claim

- **Factual and evidenced** — fine, note the evidence.
- **Factual and unevidenced** — say what evidence would be needed.
- **Health, medical or efficacy** — flag every one. These are regulated in
  most markets and the business, not the copywriter, carries the consequence.
- **Comparative** — "better than", "cheapest" — needs a substantiated basis.
- **Absolute** — "permanent", "guaranteed", "no side effects" — almost never
  supportable.

## Also check

Price claims (was/now), availability, and anything with a condition buried in
small print — a condition that changes the offer must be visible with the
offer.

## What this is not

This is a first pass to catch the obvious, not legal advice. Say so in the
output. Where something looks serious, the answer is to ask somebody
qualified, and the check should say that plainly rather than pretending to
settle it.
