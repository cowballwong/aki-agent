# Technical memo drafting

## The shape

1. **The question**, in one sentence, as asked.
2. **What we know** — facts, with their source. Documents, dates, references.
3. **What we assume** — separately, and explicitly. This section is what
   protects the memo when circumstances turn out differently.
4. **The position**, with reasoning.
5. **What would change it.**

## Rules

- Every fact traces to a document. If it came from a phone call, say so,
  with the date and who.
- Assumptions are never mixed into facts. That distinction is the whole
  professional value of the document.
- Quote codes and standards by clause and edition, from documents held — or
  say that the code has not been consulted.

## Tone

Write for the person who will read it in two years during a dispute, not for
the person who asked for it today.
