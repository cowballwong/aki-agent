# Offer and onboarding pack

## The offer

From the agreed terms, in the employer's own template. Check that what is
being offered matches what was advertised and what was discussed — the three
diverge more often than anyone expects.

Include: role, start date, pay and when it is paid, hours, probation, notice,
leave, and anything conditional (references, right to work, qualifications).

## Before day one

A checklist with owners: contract signed and returned, right-to-work
evidence, statutory enrolments, payroll set up, equipment, access, a first
week that somebody has actually planned.

In Hong Kong that includes MPF enrolment within the statutory window and
starting the employment records the Employment Ordinance requires. **Confirm
the current requirements rather than relying on this list.**

## Never

Never send an offer. Prepare it for signature by the person with authority.
