# Material order and delivery chase

## The register

What · quantity · supplier · order date · promised date · required-on-site
date · status.

The gap between the last two is the number that matters. Sort by it.

## Chasing

Say what the delay stops. "Rebar Thursday or the pour moves to next week"
gets a different answer from "please confirm delivery".

## On delivery

Check quantity and grade against the order, and record the delivery note
number. A discrepancy found at delivery is a phone call; the same discrepancy
found at installation is a programme problem.

## Watch for

- items ordered late for their lead time — flag when the order is placed, not
  when it is late
- part deliveries recorded as complete
- anything with a certificate that has not arrived with it
