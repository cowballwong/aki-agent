# Policy comparison

## Compare what matters, in this order

1. **What is covered** — perils, activities, territories, periods.
2. **What is excluded** — the section that decides most claims.
3. **Limits and sub-limits** — including the ones buried in extensions.
4. **Excess** — per claim, per event, aggregate.
5. **Conditions** — warranties and conditions precedent, which can void cover
   entirely if breached.
6. Price, last.

## Presentation

Side by side, same rows for each policy, with the wording quoted for anything
that differs materially. Quote, do not paraphrase — an exclusion paraphrased
is an exclusion misunderstood.

## Flag loudly

- cover the user thinks they have and does not
- conditions precedent they would have to actively comply with
- anything expiring or on different renewal dates

## Never

Never advise which to buy, and never state that a claim would be covered.
Show the wording; the decision is theirs or their broker's.
