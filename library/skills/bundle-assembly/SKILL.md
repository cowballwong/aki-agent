# Bundle assembly and pagination

## Order

Whatever the court, tribunal or client requires — read that first and follow
it exactly. Where nothing is specified: index, then chronological.

## The index

Item · date · description · page. Generated from the bundle itself, never
typed separately. An index that disagrees with the bundle is worse than none,
and typing it by hand is how they come to disagree.

## Pagination

Continuous, one sequence, on every page, in a position that does not obscure
anything. Never renumber after the bundle has been sent — issue a supplement
instead, or every reference anyone has made becomes wrong.

## Checks before it goes

- every document in the index is in the bundle, and vice versa
- no duplicates, unless deliberate and noted
- everything legible; illegible pages replaced or flagged
- privileged or irrelevant material removed, with somebody's confirmation
