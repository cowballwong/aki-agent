# Consultant and approvals chase

## The list

Who · what they owe · promised when · how long overdue · what it is blocking.

The last column is the one that changes behaviour. An item that blocks a
submission is not the same as one that blocks a drawing nobody needs yet, and
a chaser that says which is which gets answered.

## Chasing well

Short, specific, and with the consequence stated once: what is needed, by
when, and what slips otherwise. Draft it; the user sends it.

Escalate only on their instruction. They know the relationship.

## Authorities

Track submission date, expected response window, and the date it actually
came back. Over a few jobs this becomes a real estimate of how long that
authority takes, which is worth more than any published target.

## Never

Never chase somebody who has already answered — check the record first. A
duplicate chaser costs more goodwill than the item is worth.
