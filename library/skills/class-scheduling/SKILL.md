# Class and room scheduling

## Constraints first

Room capacity, tutor availability, student availability, travel time between
sites, and anything fixed. Write them down before scheduling — a timetable
built without them looks fine and fails in week one.

## Building it

Place the hardest constraints first. Show what could not be placed and why,
rather than dropping it quietly.

## Make-up lessons

Track owed lessons per student: what was missed, why, whether it is owed, and
when it is scheduled. This is the thing that generates disputes with parents,
and a clean record ends them.

## Output

The timetable, the unplaceable list with the reason, and the make-up ledger.
