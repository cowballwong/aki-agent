# Teaching material to a level

## Before writing anything

Ask, or take from the record: the level, the syllabus, and what the student
has already met. Material pitched half a level wrong is worse than none — it
either teaches nothing or destroys confidence.

## What to produce

- worked examples first, showing the reasoning, not just the answer
- practice graded from straightforward to stretching
- an answer key **with the working**, so a parent or tutor can see where a
  student went wrong

## Rules

- Use the notation and terminology of the syllabus they sit, not a different
  system's.
- Where a question needs real-world context, use Hong Kong contexts —
  familiar places, currency, units.
- Never present a generated question as coming from a past paper.
