# Charge and invoice reconciliation

## Compare, line by line

Quoted rate · invoiced rate · quantity basis · surcharges · currency and rate
of exchange used.

Surcharges are where the differences live: fuel, security, congestion,
documentation, handling. Check each against what was quoted or the tariff in
force on the date.

## Rules

- Check the **date** the rate applied. Tariffs change and an invoice at the
  new rate for an old booking is a common and legitimate query.
- Check the basis: weight versus volumetric, per container versus per
  shipment. Most disputes are basis, not price.
- Never adjust a figure to make a total agree.

## Output

Differences with money attached, largest first, each with the two sources
quoted, and a drafted query for the ones worth raising.
