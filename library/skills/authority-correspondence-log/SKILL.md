# Authority correspondence log

## Each entry

Date · department · reference number · in or out · subject · what it asks or
answers · response due · status.

## Why a log and not a mail folder

Because the question that gets asked is "what did they say about X, and when
did we reply" — and answering it from a mailbox takes an afternoon. The
reference number is the key; record it every time, exactly.

## Watch

- responses due, with the deadline the letter itself sets
- anything that changes a previous position — those are the ones people
  forget, and they change what is approved
- letters received and never answered

## Output

Outstanding first. Then a chronological thread per subject, which is what is
wanted when a matter is escalated.
