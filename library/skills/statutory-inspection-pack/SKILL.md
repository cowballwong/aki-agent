# Statutory inspection pack

## First, read the notice

The notice itself sets the scope, the deadline and the form of the response.
Quote it. Requirements differ between schemes and between notices, and working
from a remembered version of the scheme is how a submission is rejected.

## Build the pack

- who the qualified person is and their standing to sign
- the inspection record, in the required form
- photographs and their locations
- the works proposed, if any, and their programme
- everything the notice's own list demands

## Track

Deadline, extensions applied for, and what remains. Put the deadline on the
watch list the day the notice arrives.

## Never

Never state that a building complies. Report what was inspected and what was
found, and leave the certification to the person qualified to give it.
