# Building submission pack check

## What this does

Runs the submission against the checklist for that submission type and says
what is missing. It does **not** decide whether the design complies — that is
the certifying professional's judgement and their liability.

## Work through

- **Forms** — the right form, the current version, signed by the person with
  the standing to sign it.
- **Drawings** — the set listed on the form is the set attached; numbering and
  revisions match; the title block says what the form says.
- **Supporting documents** — calculations, reports, consents, owners'
  authorisations, anything the form's own checklist demands.
- **Consistency** — the site address, lot number and building name are
  identical everywhere they appear. Mismatches are a common rejection and a
  pure paperwork loss.

## Report

Missing first, with who has to produce each and by when. Then inconsistencies,
with both versions quoted. Then what is complete.

## Never

Never state that a design complies with a regulation. Quote what the submitted
documents say and leave the conclusion to the person who signs.
