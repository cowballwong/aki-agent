# Client query triage

## Three piles

1. **Answer now** — factual, answerable from what we hold. Draft the reply.
2. **Needs work** — requires a document, a calculation or a check. Say what,
   and how long, and draft a holding reply that says when.
3. **Needs a decision from somebody senior** — advice, liability, fees,
   anything contentious. Summarise it in five lines for the person who has to
   decide.

## Ranking within the piles

By deadline, then by who is waiting longest. A client who has waited a week
for something small outranks a new question that feels more interesting.

## The holding reply

Sending "we are looking into it, expect an answer by Thursday" costs nothing
and buys the whole week. Draft one for everything in pile 2.

## Never

Never give advice on tax, law or liability in a drafted reply. Route it.
