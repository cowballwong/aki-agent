# Safety walk record

## Each finding

Location · what was seen · risk · who it was raised with · action required ·
by when · closed date and evidence.

## Severity

Anything that could hurt somebody today goes at the top and is raised at the
time, not in a report tomorrow. Say so in the record: raised verbally to whom,
at what time.

## Close-out

A finding is closed by evidence — a photograph, a sign-off — not by time
passing. Open items carry forward to the next walk and stay visible.

## Rules

- Record what was observed. Do not name individuals for blame; record the
  condition and who is responsible for putting it right.
- Never mark an item closed on somebody's assurance without the evidence.

## Afterwards

Offer the summary for the site file and add outstanding items to the actions
list with owners.
