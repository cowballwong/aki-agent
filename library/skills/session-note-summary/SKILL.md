# Session note to progress summary

## Where it comes from

The session notes themselves, in order. Quote the observations; do not
generalise them into an impression the notes do not support.

## The shape

- what was worked on, over what period, how many sessions
- what has changed, with the observations that show it
- what has not changed
- what is planned next, and what would change the plan

## Rules

- **Distinguish observation from interpretation** in every paragraph. The
  first is the record; the second is the practitioner's, and it should be
  visibly theirs.
- Where notes are missing for a period, say so rather than smoothing the
  narrative.
- Match the reader: a referrer, a parent and a funder need different
  documents from the same notes. Ask which.

## Personal data

Stays on this machine. Anything leaving the practice is prepared for the
practitioner to review and send.
