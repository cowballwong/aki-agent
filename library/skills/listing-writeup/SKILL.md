# Property listing write-up

## Work only from the facts held

Area (and which basis — saleable, gross), floor, aspect, age, fittings,
management fee, rates, availability. If a figure is not in the record, it does
not go in the listing.

## What not to do

**Never invent or embellish a feature.** Property descriptions are regulated
in most markets and a wrong measurement or a claimed feature that does not
exist is a real liability, not a marketing flourish.

Do not describe a view, a school network or a transport time unless the user
has confirmed it as fact.

## Both languages

Write the Chinese version as a native listing, not a translation of the
English — the conventions differ. Keep numbers, areas and the address
identical across both.

## Structure

One-line headline · three or four facts that matter to a buyer · the
practical detail · what viewing requires.
