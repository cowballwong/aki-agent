# CV screening against a brief

## First, the brief

Requirements split into **must** and **prefer**, agreed before reading a
single CV. Screening without that written down is how a shortlist becomes a
set of personal preferences nobody can defend.

## For each candidate

Meets · partially meets · does not meet, per requirement, with the evidence
from the CV. Then a one-line summary and a recommendation.

## Rules that matter

- **Assess against the requirements only.** Do not comment on age, gender,
  family status, race, disability, or anything else that is neither a
  requirement nor lawful to consider. This is not only fairness — in most
  places it is the law, and a written record of an unlawful reason is
  evidence.
- Where a CV is ambiguous, say so and suggest the question, rather than
  assuming the worse reading.
- Note gaps and job-hopping as *questions to ask*, not conclusions.

## Output

Shortlist, maybes with the question that would settle each, and rejections
with a reason that could be shown to the candidate.
