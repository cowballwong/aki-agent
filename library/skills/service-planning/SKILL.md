# Service and event planning

## The running order

Time · item · who · what they need · how long. Add the transitions —
most overruns live between items, not in them.

## What is needed

Everything somebody must prepare, bring, print, set up or test, with who owns
each and when it must be ready. This list is the difference between a plan and
a hope.

## People

Who is on, who is covering, who has been asked and not replied. Chase the
last group first.

## After

Note what ran long, what did not work, and what to do differently. Written
the same day, or it will not be written — and next time the same thing
happens.
