# RFQ and supplier comparison

## Make them comparable first

Unit price is meaningless until MOQ, packing, incoterm, lead time, payment
terms and tooling costs are on the same footing. Normalise them and show the
landed cost, not the quoted price.

## The table

Supplier · unit price · MOQ · lead time · incoterm · payment terms · tooling ·
landed unit cost · what they excluded.

## What to flag

- a quote that assumed a different specification — usually the cheapest one
- payment terms that shift risk (full prepayment against a first order)
- lead times that do not fit the required date, however good the price

## Never

Do not recommend a supplier. Present the comparison and the questions to put
to each.
