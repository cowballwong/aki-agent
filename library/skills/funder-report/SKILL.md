# Funder report from service data

## Start from what was promised

The grant agreement's own outcomes and targets, quoted. Every section of the
report answers one of them. A report organised around what the organisation
did, rather than what it promised, gets sent back.

## For each outcome

Target · actual · the data behind it · what it means · what got in the way.

## Rules

- **Numbers trace to records.** A figure nobody can reproduce is the one the
  funder asks about.
- Under-performance is reported plainly with the reason. Funders continue
  funding organisations that tell them early; they stop funding surprises.
- Anonymise case examples properly — no name, no combination of details that
  identifies somebody in a small community.

## Afterwards

Put the next reporting date on the watch list, with what evidence has to be
collected between now and then.
