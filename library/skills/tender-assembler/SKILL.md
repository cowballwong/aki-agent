# Tender and bid assembler

## First, the requirements

Read the instructions to tenderers and build the list of everything demanded:
forms, certificates, insurances, CVs, examples, format, page limits, the
submission method, the deadline **and the time**. Requirements are where bids
die — not quality.

## Then, what we have

Match what the user holds against that list. Produce:

- **missing** — with who has to produce it and by when
- **out of date** — certificates and insurances expiring before the deadline
- **ready** — with where it is

## Assembling

Follow their format rules exactly, including file naming and page limits. Do
not improve on a required form.

## The deadline

Work backwards from it and say when each piece is needed, including time for
signatures and for uploading. A submission that fails on upload at 16:58 is
the most common way a good bid is lost.

## Never

Do not submit anything. Prepare it, list what is outstanding, and hand it
over.
