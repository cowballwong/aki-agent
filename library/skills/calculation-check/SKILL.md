# Calculation check

## What this is, and is not

A second reader, not a second engineer. It finds what is inconsistent,
unstated or arithmetically wrong. It does not approve a design and it does not
substitute for a competent check by somebody who carries the liability.

Say that plainly at the top of any output.

## What to look at

- **Assumptions** — are they written down, and are they used consistently
  throughout? An assumption stated on page 2 and abandoned on page 9 is the
  classic finding.
- **Loads** — every load traced from origin to foundation. Missing loads are
  far more common than wrong arithmetic.
- **Units** — consistent, and consistent with the code being used.
- **Code references** — which code, which edition, which clause.
- **Arithmetic** — spot-check, and say which you checked.

## Report

Each finding: where it is, what is wrong or unclear, and what would resolve
it. Sorted by consequence, not by page order.
