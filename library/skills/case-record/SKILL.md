# Service and case record keeping

## What a record entry contains

Date · who was present · what was observed or said · what was done · what was
agreed · next contact.

## The discipline

**Fact and observation, attributed.** "Mrs L said she had not slept" is a
record. "Mrs L is not coping" is an assessment, and belongs in a labelled
assessment section written by the person qualified to make it.

Records are read later by colleagues, by the person themselves, and sometimes
by an inquiry. Write for all three.

## Personal data

Case records are sensitive personal information. **They stay on this
machine.** Nothing is pasted into a draft message, and anything shared is
prepared deliberately with names removed unless the recipient is entitled to
them.

## Never

Never delete or rewrite a past entry. Correct it with a new dated entry that
says what was wrong.
