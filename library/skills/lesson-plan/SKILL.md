# Lesson plan from a syllabus point

## The plan

Objective in one sentence, in what the student will be able to do · the hook ·
the teaching sequence with timings · what students do · how you will know they
got it · homework if any.

## The two contingencies nobody plans and everybody needs

- **if it runs short** — the extension that is genuinely extra, not filler
- **if it runs long** — what to cut, decided now rather than at 10 minutes to
  the bell

## Materials

List everything needed, including what has to be prepared, printed or booked.
A plan that needs something nobody printed is a plan that fails at the door.

## Differentiation

Say what changes for the students who find it hard and for those who finish
early. Two lines each is enough and it is the part most plans skip.

## Language

Match the medium of instruction, and where the class is bilingual say which
parts are delivered in which language.
