# Inspection report summary

## What to extract

What was inspected, against what standard, sample size, findings by category,
and the pass or fail conclusion the report itself reaches.

Quote the conclusion; do not restate it more softly or more harshly.

## Findings

Group by whether they are: critical, major, minor — using the report's own
categories, not your own. Then say plainly what each means for shipping.

## The decision to surface

Ship, rework, re-inspect, or reject — with what each costs in time. That is
the decision the user has to make today, and the report usually buries it.

## Rules

- Never soften a critical finding.
- Where photographs support a finding, reference them.
- Where the report is ambiguous, say so and draft the question to the
  inspector.
