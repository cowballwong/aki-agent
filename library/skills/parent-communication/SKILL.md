# Parent communication

## Routine notices

Short. Date, what is happening, what the parent must do, by when. Put the
action first — a notice whose request is in the fourth paragraph does not get
actioned.

Produce both languages side by side where the school works bilingually.

## Difficult messages

Different rules, and they matter:

- **Facts before conclusions.** What happened, when, who saw it. Not what it
  says about the child.
- **One issue per message.** A list of concerns reads as an attack and gets
  answered as one.
- **Say what you are asking for** — a conversation, a change at home,
  information.
- **Never diagnose.** Describe behaviour; recommend who could assess it.

## Rules

- Never send. Everything is drafted for the teacher to review, and a
  difficult message should be read once more before it goes.
- Keep a record of what was sent and when. For anything sensitive that record
  matters later.
