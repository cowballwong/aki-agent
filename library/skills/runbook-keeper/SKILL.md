# Runbook keeper

## What a runbook contains

The thing it is for · when to use it · the steps, exactly · how to know it
worked · what to do if it did not · who to tell.

Steps are commands or clicks, not descriptions of intent. A runbook is read at
2am by somebody tired.

## Keeping it current

When the user does a recurring task in a way that differs from the runbook,
that is the moment to update it — not later. Ask, then update, and date the
change.

## What to flag

- runbooks not used in a long time, which are usually wrong
- steps that failed last time and were worked around
- anything that depends on one person's knowledge and is not written down.
  That last one is the real risk in every small operation.
