# Claim follow-up

## The record

Date of loss · date notified · claim reference · what has been submitted ·
what is outstanding · who is holding it · last chase · reserve or offer.

## Notification

The date and method of notification is the first thing to record and the
first thing anybody disputes. Record it precisely, with the evidence.

## Chasing

Chase the specific person holding the specific document. "Any update?" gets
"still with the adjuster". "Is the surveyor's report back — it was due on the
6th" gets an answer.

## What to surface

- documents outstanding from the client, which the client can fix today
- documents outstanding from the insurer, which need chasing up a chain
- how long since anything moved

## Never

Do not accept, settle or advise on quantum. Present the position.
